# SwitchWithAI

AI resume tailoring for job seekers in India. Upload your DOCX, paste a job description, and get your own resume back with the role's ATS keywords added in context. Your formatting kept, nothing invented.

- Website: https://www.switchwithai.com/
- MCP server: https://www.switchwithai.com/mcp-server
- Chrome extension: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/switchwithai-overlay/clmmpfgcafaoadjcmdgdgjcipfbkpoib
